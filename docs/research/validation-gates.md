# Validation gates before product investment

The technical prototype is not evidence of demand. Before broadening scope, obtain
repeated, quantified answers from platform, security, and audit teams:

1. Do they already correlate approval, identity, plan, API mutations, and runtime
   results?
2. Has failure to correlate them caused an audit finding, incident, or material
   engineering cost?
3. Which evidence do auditors or internal security teams reject today?
4. Would the organization place another service on the production authorization
   path?
5. Is Kubernetes, Terraform/AWS, or release-artifact evidence the highest-cost
   target?
6. What engineering time and vendor spend does the current control consume?
7. Can current CI, PAM, and deployment vendors close the gap through configuration?
8. Would the control be mandatory infrastructure or an optional dashboard?

Until those questions produce repeated evidence, the technical gap survives but
the broad startup thesis does not.
